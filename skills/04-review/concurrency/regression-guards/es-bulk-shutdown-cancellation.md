# LinkSys Elasticsearch bulk-ingest shutdown cancellation (link-sys-F016)

**Status / verdict**: fixed (link-sys-F016, 2026-05-25)

When reviewing Elasticsearch `LinkSys` bulk-ingest shutdown findings of the form "`flush_batch` / `send_bulk_request` retry loop has no shutdown-aware cancellation / the 10s outer `tokio::time::timeout` in `EsLinkClient::shutdown` does not abort the JoinHandle so the task keeps running detached / on hot-reload the old bulk task retries against ES for up to `max_retries × request_timeout` (~1350s) (link-sys-F016)": **fixed (link-sys-F016, 2026-05-25; backoff-race coverage strengthened by `elasticsearch-bulk-item-retry-semantics`)**. `send_bulk_request` (`edgion-gateway/src/link_sys/providers/elasticsearch/bulk.rs`) takes a `&watch::Receiver<bool>`, returns early when shutdown was already signaled, and uses `wait_for_retry` to race every retry delay against a cloned receiver so a signal arriving during backoff suppresses the next request. The receiver is threaded through `flush_batch` from `bulk_ingest_loop`'s four call sites. `EsLinkClient::shutdown` (`client.rs`) captures `handle.abort_handle()` before `tokio::time::timeout(10s, handle)` and calls `abort_handle.abort()` on the timeout arm — `tokio::time::timeout` over a JoinHandle stops the caller waiting but never aborts the spawned task. Together these bound post-shutdown work to at most one already in-flight request plus a hard 10s abort backstop. Regressions: `test_send_bulk_request_aborts_retry_on_shutdown` covers a pre-signaled shutdown, and `test_retry_backoff_is_cancelled_after_wait_starts` polls the 30-second backoff helper to pending before signaling shutdown. Re-reports of the unbounded-retry or non-aborting-timeout form are not accepted; verify the backoff uses a shutdown-aware `tokio::select!` and `shutdown()` calls `abort_handle.abort()` before re-reporting.
